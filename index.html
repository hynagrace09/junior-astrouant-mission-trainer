Mars Outpost Trainer

Words to know

Sol: one day on Mars. It lasts about 24 hours and 40 minutes.

Radiation: invisible energy from the Sun and space that can harm astronauts.

Solar flare: a sudden burst of energy and radiation from the Sun.

Shield: thick walls or soil around the base that block radiation.

Where does the science come from?

Earth can’t help instantly. In real life, radio messages take minutes to reach Mars and supply ships take months. This game shortens that to 2 sols so you can feel the wait.
